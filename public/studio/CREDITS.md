## Two are cropped, and the crop is the point

`garment-rack.jpg` and `folded-stack.jpg` are **not** the full frames. Both
originals carry a legible third-party garment brand on the neck label —
`GRACECHAPEL` with its URL on the rack, `BLOOK® PREMIUM BRAND REG.TM` on every
tee in the stack. A merch company showing another apparel brand's blanks as its
own is an own-goal, and a real organisation's merch on this site reads as a
client we do not have — the fabrication `QuoteCards` and `/work` both refuse in
prose. Re-deriving either file from its original will put that branding back.

Rejected for the same reason: a delivery-van shot whose every parcel was
Zalando- or Amazon-branded, and a rack of tie-dye one-offs carrying other
people's printed artwork.
